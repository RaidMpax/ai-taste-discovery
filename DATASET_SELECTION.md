# V2 Dataset Selection

Status: editorial proposal for review. This is not the final 500-album list.

## Editorial direction

The V2 catalogue should feel like a contemporary record and magazine shop:
rooted in current Western pop culture and K-pop, but extending toward critically
respected indie, electronic, R&B, hip-hop, and canonical albums. Breadth of
artists matters more than collecting complete discographies.

Target: roughly 500 albums from 280–320 artists.

## Album-count rules

- New or currently rising artist: usually 1 album, occasionally 2.
- Established contemporary artist: usually 1–2 albums.
- Canonical artist: usually 2–3 albums, never a full discography.
- Pink Floyd: at most 2 representative albums.
- Sabrina Carpenter: only the releases from her culturally relevant breakthrough.
- EPs and mini albums are allowed when they are central to an artist, especially
  in K-pop; they are not accepted merely to increase the count.

Default exclusions:

- deluxe/remastered duplicates;
- ordinary compilations and greatest-hits packages;
- live, remix, demo, and bootleg releases;
- weak early work retained only because the artist later became popular;
- albums with chart visibility but little editorial or recommendation value.

Explicit artist exclusions requested by the product owner:

- Ed Sheeran
- 周杰伦
- K-pop boy groups
- Hong Kong/Taiwan male pop singers such as 陳奕迅 and 張國榮

## Approximate balance

- Western artists: 70–75%
- K-pop and Korean artists: about 15%
- Hong Kong/Taiwan artists: small, selective representation
- Japanese, Latin, African, and other global exceptions: about 10%
- Releases from 2010 onward: about 55–60%

These are guardrails, not quotas that justify a weak album.

## Borderline decisions for product review

The product owner explicitly confirmed that Taylor Swift, Ariana Grande, Lady
Gaga, Miley Cyrus, The Weeknd, The 1975, Future, Playboi Carti, and Travis
Scott may remain, subject to album-level selection.

The following require album-level curation rather than automatic discography
inclusion:

- very large mainstream catalogues, even when the artist is approved;
- artists whose cultural relevance may be stronger than album consistency;
- artists with significant controversy where inclusion is an editorial rather
  than technical decision;
- artists whose influence is clear but whose later catalogue is inconsistent.

Kanye West and Drake were added to the slate after the product owner delegated
the remaining choices and accepted the defaults in `EDITORIAL_DECISIONS.md`.
This changes the working artist total from 280 to 282.
