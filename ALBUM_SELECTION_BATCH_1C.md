# Album Selection Batch 1C: K-pop Girl Groups and Female Soloists

Status: curated longlist for product-owner review. Nothing here has been
ingested.

This batch treats albums and substantial mini albums as valid projects, but
does not attempt to preserve every comeback. Japanese-language duplicates,
single albums, remix editions, and routine repackages are excluded by default.

## Product-owner decisions

- Retain i-dle - `2` for its distinct later-era songs and member production.
- Retain ARTMS - `<Club Icarus>`.
- Retain BLACKPINK - `DEADLINE` despite its short runtime.
- Exclude BoA - `Kiss My Lips`; `No.1` is sufficient at this catalogue size.
- Retain Girls' Generation - `The Boys`.
- Retain IVE - `IVE EMPATHY`.
- Retain IU - `Love poem` for its distinct ballad and fan-favourite era.
- Retain LE SSERAFIM - `CRAZY`.
- Retain LOONA - `[12:00]` for the internationally visible "Why Not?" and
  "Star" era.
- Exclude tripleS - `ASSEMBLE25`; it adds too little beyond `ASSEMBLE24`.
- Exclude TWICE - `THIS IS FOR`; the earlier selected albums are stronger.
- Retain XG - `NEW DNA`.
- Retain Yerin Baek - `tellusboutyourself` for its electronic contrast.

## Format and identity notes

- `(G)I-DLE` is stored under the current artist name `i-dle`; MusicBrainz artist
  identity resolution must preserve the same underlying artist.
- LOONA and Red Velvet repackages are explicit exceptions because their added
  title tracks define major eras. Other routine repackages remain excluded.
- BLACKPINK's `DEADLINE` is a five-track mini album, not a full album.
- Japanese-language duplicates and aespa's Japanese albums are outside this
  Korean-catalogue batch.
- LOONA inclusion is metadata coverage only and does not require linking users
  to a particular streaming platform or rights holder.
