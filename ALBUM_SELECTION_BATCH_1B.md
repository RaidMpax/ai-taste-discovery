## Product-owner decisions

- Exclude Ethel Cain - `Perverts`.
- Exclude KATSEYE - `SIS (Soft Is Strong)`.
- Exclude Sabrina Carpenter - `emails i can't send`.
- Retain Lola Young - `I'm Only F**king Myself`.
- Retain Olivia Dean - `Messy`.
- Retain The Marías - `CINEMA`.
- Exclude Ella Langley - `hungover`; `Dandelion` is sufficient current coverage.
- Exclude Gracie Abrams - `Good Riddance`; the product owner dislikes the artist
  and `The Secret of Us` is sufficient coverage.
- Retain Tate McRae - `THINK LATER` as seed-only coverage for the "greedy" era.
- Retain The Last Dinner Party - `From the Pyre` as a worthwhile darker second
  album rather than a duplicate of the debut.

## Format notes

- `Perverts` is treated as a substantial experimental project rather than a
  routine EP.
- KATSEYE's short EPs are eligible because the group does not yet have a studio
  album and each EP represents a public era.
- PinkPantheress mixtapes are treated as central projects, not miscellaneous
  compilations.
- Standard releases are used instead of deluxe or remix expansions.
