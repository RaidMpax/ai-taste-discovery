# V2 Album Selection Balance Audit

Status: final editorial pass before MusicBrainz canonical matching and database
ingestion.

## Outcome

- Releases before Batch 4: 466
- Batch 4 under untrimmed defaults: 86
- Untrimmed total: 552
- Secondary default slots returned: 22
- Final accepted total: 530
- Final artist targets represented: 282 of 282

The audit removes no artist and overrides none of the product owner's explicit
album-level keep decisions. It reduces only default depth allocations.

## What this audit does not mean

These 22 releases are not judged bad. They are deferred because a 530-release
catalogue needs breadth across artists more than additional within-artist depth.
They remain documented candidates for a later expansion.
